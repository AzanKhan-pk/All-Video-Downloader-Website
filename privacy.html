Privacy statement

Your privacy matters.

This website is designed as a simple static download page and does not require an account to use its download links.

Information on this website

This website does not ask visitors to create an account or enter passwords. It does not intentionally collect personal information through the static pages themselves.

Download links

APK and Windows download buttons point to GitHub Releases. When you follow those links, GitHub may process the request according to GitHub's own privacy practices and policies.

Contact information

If you choose to contact the developer through WhatsApp or Gmail, the information you send is handled by those services and by the recipient. Do not send passwords, payment details, or other sensitive information in feedback messages.

Third-party services

The website may contact GitHub's public release API to display the latest release version. External services have their own policies and may process normal web requests, including technical information needed to provide their services.

Updates to this statement

This privacy statement may be updated when the website's features or data practices change. The latest version will be published on this page.
