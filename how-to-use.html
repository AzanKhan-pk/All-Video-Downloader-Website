Quick guide

How to use it.

Follow these simple steps to install the app and download supported public media.

01

Choose your device

Open the home page and choose Download APK for Android or Download EXE for Windows.

02

Install the app

Install the downloaded package on your device. On Windows, run the installer and follow the normal installation steps.

03

Copy a public media link

Copy the URL of supported public media that you have permission to save, then paste it into the downloader.

04

Select quality

Choose from the resolutions that are actually available for that media. Higher resolutions such as 1080p, 2K or 4K may not be available for every video.

05

Start the download

Start the download and wait for the app to finish. Download speed depends on your connection and the source.

06

Need help?

If you find a problem, use the Contact page to send feedback or a bug report through WhatsApp or Gmail.

i

Important

Only download media you have permission to save and follow the rules and terms of the platform hosting the media.
